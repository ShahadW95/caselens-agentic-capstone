## Fabricated client statements and trade confirmations

The SEC documented that BLMIS produced fabricated client account statements and trade
confirmations describing purchases and sales that never occurred at the reported scale, and
that the firm generated computer-produced records to support this fiction
[SRC_SEC_2009_FABRICATED_RECORDS].

## The purported auditor

The SEC also brought an action concerning the purported auditor of BLMIS, addressing
accountability of related parties involved in sustaining the appearance of a legitimate,
audited investment business [SRC_SEC_2009_AUDITOR_ACTION].

## SEC OIG: documented complaints, examinations, and missed opportunities

The SEC Office of Inspector General documented that the SEC received complaints, conducted
examinations, and had opportunities to independently verify Madoff's claimed trading
activity before the scheme's 2008 collapse, but did not do so
[SRC_SEC_OIG_REPORT] [SRC_SEC_OIG_TESTIMONY].

## Fact vs. claim discipline

- **Documented fact:** "The SEC received complaints about Madoff's operation and had
  opportunities to independently verify his claimed trading that were not acted on before
  the scheme's collapse" [SRC_SEC_OIG_REPORT] [SRC_SEC_OIG_TESTIMONY].
- **Unsupported claim:** "The SEC was corrupt or bribed." No source in this pack supports
  corruption or bribery; the SEC OIG record documents institutional and procedural failure,
  not intentional misconduct. This digest and downstream specialists must not upgrade a
  documented process failure into an accusation of corruption.

## Did Madoff act completely alone?

Madoff's own guilty plea establishes his own criminal liability for the scheme
[SRC_DOJ_SDNY_CASEPAGE]. The SEC's separate action concerning the purported auditor shows
that at least one other party faced regulatory scrutiny connected to the scheme's
appearance of legitimacy [SRC_SEC_2009_AUDITOR_ACTION]. Other individuals connected to
BLMIS were subject to separate proceedings that are outside the core facts of this case
pack. None of this implies that all BLMIS staff, family members, feeder funds, or advisors
had knowledge of the fraud; knowledge-by-association is not supported by any source in this
pack and must not be asserted.
