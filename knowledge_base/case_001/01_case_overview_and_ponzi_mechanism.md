## Who and what BLMIS was

Bernard L. Madoff Investment Securities LLC (BLMIS) was a New York investment-advisory
business run by Bernard Madoff [SRC_DOJ_SDNY_CASEPAGE]. Under the guise of a proprietary
"split-strike conversion" options strategy, BLMIS told clients it was buying baskets of
S&P 100 stocks while hedging with index options, and reported steady, unusually consistent
returns [SRC_FBI_HISTORY].

## The fabrication mechanism

In reality, no such trading occurred at the scale reported. Client account statements,
trade confirmations, and reports describing this strategy were fabricated
[SRC_DOJ_SDNY_CASEPAGE] [SRC_FBI_HISTORY]. New client deposits were used to pay purported
returns and honor withdrawal requests for earlier investors — the defining mechanism of a
Ponzi scheme [SRC_FBI_HISTORY]. The scheme required continuous fabrication of paper records
because no underlying investment activity generated the reported performance
[SRC_DOJ_SDNY_CASEPAGE].

## Duration

Official sources describe the fraud as running "for decades" rather than asserting one
precise start year; this digest does not assert a single exact start date beyond what
DOJ's case materials support [SRC_DOJ_SDNY_CASEPAGE]. Narrower, specifically sourced
milestones (regulatory actions, plea, sentencing) are recorded separately in
`02_verified_timeline.md`.

## Madoff's actual industry standing — not a government official

Madoff was never a government official. He held prominent private securities-industry and
self-regulatory-organization (SRO) roles, including positions associated with NASD and
NASDAQ, and he participated in an SEC advisory committee
[SRC_FINRA_TESTIMONY] [SRC_SEC_OIG_TESTIMONY]. These were private-sector and SRO
positions, not government employment, and this digest does not characterize that
institutional access as bribery, conspiracy, or government employment absent a cited
source, consistent with the source policy.

## Scope note on related prosecutions

Other individuals connected to BLMIS were subject to separate proceedings. Those related
prosecutions are outside the core facts of this case pack and are referenced only where an
official record is needed to evaluate the narrow question of whether Madoff acted
completely alone (see `03_claims_red_flags_and_records.md`). They are not treated as
separate cases here [SRC_DOJ_SDNY_CASEPAGE].
